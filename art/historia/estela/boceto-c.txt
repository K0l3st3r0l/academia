Character design of Estela, the mysterious and epic guardian of time, astronomer of a magical observatory outside time. Ageless woman, serene and powerful but friendly, eyes that glow softly with tiny galaxies, long hair that turns into a flowing night sky with stars at the tips, one silver streak. Her long cape is a living starry sky of the Atacama desert with the Southern Cross and the dark llama-shaped cloud of the Milky Way, with small golden clock gears floating around her. A brass astrolabe on her chest. Elegant deep indigo and gold outfit. Same art style as a cheerful kids' educational game: flat vector cartoon illustration, thick dark navy outlines (#1A1A2E), flat colours with soft simple shading, friendly rounded shapes, slightly stylised proportions (big expressive eyes, head a bit large), full body standing, front three-quarter view, plain flat light background, no text.
