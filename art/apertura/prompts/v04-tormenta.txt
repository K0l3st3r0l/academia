A storm of time bursts into the peaceful observatory hall: violet clouds and lightning swirl across the round window, the lanterns flicker, wind rushes through the ivy, and the great clock shakes, cracks down the middle and its four glowing medallions shoot out through the window like falling stars, leaving dark empty holes and loose floating gears. Dramatic but not scary, suitable for young children. No dialogue and no narration: only wind, a rumble of thunder, a cracking sound and dramatic orchestral music. Soft painted 2D cartoon animation.
